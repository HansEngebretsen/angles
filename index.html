Angles

Turns a screenshot or screen recording into a calm, photographic camera move: a subtle angle, real depth of field and a screen that looks photographed, for product demos and portfolio headers.

  • Pick a style and a motion level, and it moves slowly and well on its own.
  • Set focus and position at any frame; between keyframes it tweens.
  • Add shortcuts or captions that sit above the camera and the look.
  • Trim, split and stitch clips, crop the source, pick a frame shape.
  • Export a video, or snapshot a frame at full size.
